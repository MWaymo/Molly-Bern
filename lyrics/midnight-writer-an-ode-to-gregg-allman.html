Midnight Writer

(An Ode to Gregg Allman)

Listen on Suno →

The house goes dead and the clock hands crawl
Moonlight bleeds on the kitchen wall
Chewed-up pen and on my last bar
Bottling lightning in a mason jar.

Don’t let ’em catch the Midnight Writer
Burning the midnight oil, but the flame gets brighter
Early birds can get the worm
She’ll write the words on her own terms.

The sweater’s secondhand, the pen’s from the bank
Napkin for paper, and the rent’s a blank
Pockets hold receipts and a grudge or two
Broke as a joke, but the rhymes cut through.

Don’t let ’em catch the Midnight Writer
Burning the midnight oil, but the flame gets brighter
Early birds can get the worm
She’ll write the words on her own terms.

The stars are punctures in a sheet of black
Comets are sentences that don’t look back
Crickets keep time in the cooling dew
And the old owl smirks like he’s daring you.

Writer’s block rides in with a sheriff’s grin
Cold cuffs ready, says, “Let the quiet in,”
But I slip every cuff with a rhyme and a wink
Leave the law standing there, out of ink.

Now it’s noon, the pages are written
The blues are a-snooze but nothing is hidden
So don’t let ’em catch the Midnight Writer
Don’t let ’em catch the Midnight Writer.
