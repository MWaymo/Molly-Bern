About

Smoky voice, sharp tongue, long road.

Molly Bern writes dark Americana for people who’ve been through it and have a few things to say. Her songs pair smoky, weathered vocals with slide guitar, piano, mandolin, and upright bass. They’re built on tight rhymes and plain talk, and they swing between confession and swagger.

Her debut album, Zero Percent Contained, is a twenty-song run through fever, fire, and fallout. It’s gritty, funny, and not interested in being contained.
